\begin{abstract}
This report consolidates a ten-part research programme on small,
recurrent language models into a single volume. The empirical
centerpiece is a loop-depth extrapolation study on a 44M-parameter
recurrent mixture-of-experts transformer trained on TinyStories: test-time
gains saturate at the trained loop depth and slightly degrade beyond it.
Around this result, the report develops the
architecture analysis (sparse routing, grouped-query attention,
depth--width tradeoffs), training methodology (curriculum design,
loop-aware distillation), and evaluation and deployment concerns
(test-time compute taxonomy, quantization, mechanistic probes, and a
benchmark proposal) for sub-billion-parameter recurrent models. Proposed
experiments are labelled as such throughout; the only executed study is
the loop sweep of Section~3.
\end{abstract}
\bigskip
\end{@twocolumnfalse}
